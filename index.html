Educator Notes: AI & Pedagogy

This application integrates 50 Core Artificial Intelligence Concepts designed to gently introduce the foundations of Applied Intelligent Systems to 4-to-6-year-olds.

It utilizes Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) by John Sweller to make advanced concepts accessible:

  • Minimizing Extraneous Load: Interfaces are stripped of unnecessary text. The scrolling layout enforces a vertical flex-column to ensure tactile mobile behavior and guaranteed option visibility.
  • Managing Intrinsic Load: Complex technical ideas (e.g. Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Algorithmic Bias, Deepfakes) are reduced to highly relatable, safe, single-step ECE trivia schemas.
  • Modality Effect: The Text-to-Speech (TTS) feature prevents working memory overload by offloading reading demands to the auditory channel.
